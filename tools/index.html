Which commission tool should you use?

Use the monthly commission calculator when you already know the main terms of one pay plan and want to estimate a check. It is best for questions like, “What does 14 units at this gross and rate look like?” or “How much is pack reducing my average per car?”

Use the pay-plan comparison calculator when a manager has offered a different plan, you are considering another store, or the higher percentage is paired with a higher pack. It separates regular deals from likely minis and calculates each plan under the same month, making a structural difference easier to see.

Use the free deal tracker throughout the month. A calculator is only as good as the averages you enter. Recording each unit's gross, commission, delivery status, and paperwork creates the independent history you need to replace a guess with your own numbers.

How these tools handle your data

The calculators run in your browser and do not submit the numbers you enter to a CarSales Tracker Pro server. The free web tracker also stores its deal data in the browser. That local-first design means no login is required, but it also means the site cannot recover your information after browser storage is cleared. Use the export function in the tracker settings to keep a backup.

What the estimates cannot know

No public calculator can see your dealership's internal cost, accounting adjustments, written pay plan, payroll deductions, or chargeback rules. The tools model the assumptions you enter; they do not determine wages or legal rights. Compare the result with your signed plan and official statement, then ask payroll or a qualified professional about a material disagreement.
