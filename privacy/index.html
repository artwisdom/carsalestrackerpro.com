Privacy Policy

This policy explains what CarSales Tracker Pro collects, what it doesn't, and the choices you have. The short version: the deals you log in the free web tracker stay in your own browser, and the rest of this page covers the cookies, advertising, and analytics that apply to the website itself.

This Privacy Policy applies to the website carsalestrackerpro.com (the "Site") and the free web tracker it hosts, operated by Andromeda Kinship ("we," "us," or "our"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Site.

The data you enter in the tracker stays on your device

The free web tracker is designed to work entirely inside your browser. The deals, commissions, delivery statuses, and settings you enter are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. This information is not transmitted to us, is not stored on a server we control, and is not associated with any account. Because of this:

  • We cannot see, access, or recover your tracker data.
  • Clearing your browser data, using private/incognito mode, or switching browsers or devices will remove or separate that data. Use the built-in Export data and Import data tools to move it.
  • You do not need to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or dealership to use the tracker.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and the third-party services described below may automatically receive standard technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the pages you view. This information is used to operate, secure, and improve the Site, and (as described below) to serve and measure advertising.

Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file placed on your device. We and our third-party partners use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand how the Site is used, and — where applicable — deliver and measure advertising. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; note that disabling cookies may affect how parts of the Site function.

Advertising, including Google AdSense

We may display third-party advertising on the Site, including through Google AdSense. The following disclosures apply:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this Site and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie), web beacons, and device identifiers to collect information in the course of serving and measuring ads.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices (or youronlinechoices.eu in the EU).
  • For more on how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertising and its cookies are provided by third parties and are governed by their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics and Google Search Console to understand aggregate traffic — for example, which pages are popular and how visitors find us. This helps us decide what to write and improve. Analytics data is used in aggregate and is not used by us to personally identify you.

We use the limited information described above to operate and secure the Site, understand and improve how it's used, serve and measure advertising, respond to messages you send us, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell the personal information of our visitors.

The Site links to third-party sites, including the Apple App Store, BenchCalcs, and others. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of sites we don't operate. Review their policies before providing them information.

The Site is intended for professionals and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will address it.

Your rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information:

  • EEA / UK (GDPR): rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because the tracker's data lives only on your device, much of this is under your direct control; for anything the Site's advertising or analytics partners hold, use the opt-out links above and their own tools.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To limit ad personalization, use the opt-out links in the Advertising section above.

To make a request or ask a question about your data, email us at support@andromedakinship.com.

Data retention & security

We retain the minimal information we receive only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Site or applicable law changes. We'll revise the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, make it more prominent. Continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
