Used EV Prices Jump 9.8% This Fall

Dealer Ops Daily · Tuesday, November 3, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Used EV values just outpaced every other segment on your lot — and year-end volume is exactly when a bad title slips through unnoticed. Here's what to check before you sign today's deals.

☕ HUDDLE STARTER · read it aloud in 60 seconds

9.8% — Used electric vehicles are worth 9.8% more than they were a year ago — almost double what hybrids gained. If you've got EV inventory, you don't need to be the first one to cut price this week.

Ask the team: Who's got a used EV sitting past 45 days that we should re-price instead of just discounting?

⚖️ Protect the deal

Year-End Volume Could Let Title Fraud Slip Through

According to Digital Dealer (Nov. 2, 2026), a mismatch between the name on a trade's title and the person signing the deal is the kind of shortcut that gets waved through during year-end volume — then freezes that unit out of your used inventory come January. Not yet independently verified by a second outlet.

Use it today: Before a trade heads to the back room, check that the title name matches the signer. If it doesn't, flag your F&I manager before the paperwork gets signed, not after delivery.

Sources: Digital Dealer / Dealer Magazine

💰 The desk & the box

What the Best F&I Managers Track Beyond the Numbers

According to F&I and Showroom (Nov. 1, 2026), the strongest F&I managers measure the behaviors behind their PVR, not just the final figure — things like whether every customer actually got a full menu presentation versus a rushed pitch. Not yet independently verified by a second outlet.

Use it today: Ask your F&I manager this week what share of last month's deals got a full menu presentation. That ratio predicts next month's PVR better than the total does, and it's the kind of question that gets you noticed for the box.

Sources: F&I and Showroom (FI Magazine)

🚗 Market numbers

Used EV Prices Up 9.8% Since Last September

Used EV prices climbed 9.8% year-over-year as of September, nearly double the pace of hybrids, according to Car Dealership Guy News (Nov. 1, 2026). Not yet independently verified by a second outlet.

Use it today: If you're sitting on used EV inventory, you've got room to hold your price this week instead of discounting first. Pull your EV aging report today and see what's actually sitting past 45 days.

Sources: Car Dealership Guy News

New-Vehicle Inventory Ticks Up, But MY2026s Are Piling Up

According to F&I and Showroom (Nov. 2, 2026), September new-vehicle inventory rose slightly month-over-month, but lots remain loaded with MY2026 units as the model-year changeover drags on longer than usual.

Use it today: Pull your oldest MY2026 units today and ask the desk about incentive support before you quote a customer — those units likely have more room to move than your sheet shows.

Sources: F&I and Showroom (FI Magazine)

🏬 The floor

Asbury's DMS Switch Is Easing Margin Pressure

According to Automotive Dive (Nov. 2, 2026), Asbury's shift to the Tekion DMS platform has boosted efficiency and streamlined deal flow, which is easing pressure to cut margins even as off-lease inventory climbs.

Use it today: If your store is still on a legacy DMS, ask your GM what's actually slowing deal turnaround — a faster system means fewer minutes per deal and more ups worked per shift.

Sources: Automotive Dive
