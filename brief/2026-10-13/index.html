Family Ford Store Sells As EV Objections Shift

Dealer Ops Daily · Tuesday, October 13, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

A family-owned Ford store just traded hands to a consolidator, and the EV conversation on your lot is flipping from 'how far can it go' to 'how good does it feel to drive.' Here's what changes your talk track today.

💰 The desk & the box

Wise F&I's Amy Counts named 2026 NAMAD Woman of the Year

According to AutoSuccess (Oct. 12, 2026), Wise F&I's Amy Counts won NAMAD's 2026 Woman of the Year award for advocacy on behalf of minority dealers and long-running dealer partnerships. Not independently verified beyond this report.

Use it today: If the box is your next move, ask your F&I manager how dealer-advocacy work like Counts' factors into getting noticed for promotion.

Sources: AutoSuccess

🚗 Market numbers

EV buyers want 4x the range they'll actually use

A BMW-commissioned survey reported by Automotive Dive (Oct. 12, 2026) found 75 miles of range covers daily driving for nearly three-quarters of drivers, yet 42% still want at least 300 miles before buying an EV. Separately, F&I and Showroom (Oct. 12, 2026) reports the EV sales conversation has shifted from 'should I buy one' to 'which features do I want.'

Use it today: Stop opening EV pitches with range numbers — lead with features and ownership experience, then use range as the reassurance that closes, not the hook that opens.

Sources: Automotive Dive · F&I and Showroom

Stellantis reportedly bringing back a V-8 Hellcat Charger

According to Automotive News (Oct. 12, 2026), Stellantis is planning a V-8 Hellcat version of the Dodge Charger. Not yet independently verified beyond this report, and no launch date or pricing has been confirmed.

Use it today: If a Charger shopper is hesitating on the current lineup, mention the reported V-8 Hellcat is coming — it can turn 'I'll wait and see' into a deposit on today's inventory.

Sources: Automotive News

🏬 The floor

O'Brien Auto Team buys Jack Kain Ford in Kentucky

According to CBT News (Oct. 12, 2026), O'Brien Auto Team acquired Jack Kain Ford in Versailles, Kentucky, from Bob Kain, Vickie Kain Fister, and Pat Kain. Not independently verified beyond this report.

Use it today: If your store changes hands, get pay plan and book-of-business terms in writing from the new GM in your first week — don't wait for the rumor mill to fill in the gaps.

Sources: CBT News

Stellantis joins ASE Connects to build technician pipelines

According to AutoSuccess (Oct. 12, 2026), Stellantis became an OEM Elite Partner on ASE Connects, giving dealers discounted platform access, school partnerships, and Mopar CAP alignment to recruit technicians using local workforce data. Not independently verified beyond this report.

Use it today: If a customer worries about service wait times after the sale, mention your store's growing tech pipeline — it's a real signal service capacity is catching up to demand.

Sources: AutoSuccess
