Penske Goes Private at $16.3B Valuation

Dealer Ops Daily · Monday, October 19, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Penske Automotive is going private in a $16.3 billion buyout by Mitsui & Co. and Penske Corp., the biggest ownership move of the week. Meanwhile, NHTSA quietly dropped its 2027 crash-test list — here's what changes on your lot before either story reaches your next up.

⚖️ Protect the deal

NHTSA releases its Model Year 2027 crash-test list for 5-star ratings

NHTSA published its Model Year 2027 vehicle testing list, naming which new models the agency will crash-test for its 5-Star Safety Ratings program, according to the agency's own release (Oct. 18, 2026). The list determines which vehicles get an official government safety score before they reach dealer lots next year.

Use it today: Check whether your incoming 2027 models made the list — a confirmed test means a rating is coming, so don't promise a star rating on a model that isn't on it yet.

Sources: NHTSA

💰 The desk & the box

Lenders are tightening fraud checks as auto lending goes digital

Digital Dealer reports that auto lenders are seeing more sophisticated, automated fraud attempts as lending platforms modernize, raising new security questions for the systems that process deal paperwork (Digital Dealer, Oct. 16, 2026). Synthetic-identity and document fraud are flagged as the fastest-growing risks for lenders connected to dealer systems.

Use it today: If a deal flags for an extra ID-verification stip you haven't seen before, don't treat it as a red flag on your buyer — it's likely a lender's new fraud check, not a problem with your paperwork.

Sources: Digital Dealer

🚗 Market numbers

Pull NHTSA complaint records before you appraise your next trade

Digital Dealer highlights a free resource most used-car departments skip: NHTSA's public owner-complaint and recall database, searchable by year, make and model (Digital Dealer, Oct. 15, 2026; NHTSA.gov). Cross-checking a trade against open complaints can flag issues before they surface in a vehicle history report.

Use it today: Before you commit to a trade number, run the year/make/model through NHTSA's complaint search — a pattern of owner complaints is a reconditioning cost you can build into your offer now instead of eating later.

Sources: Digital Dealer · NHTSA.gov

🏬 The floor

Penske Automotive goes private in $16.3B buyout

Mitsui & Co. and Penske Corp. are buying out Penske Automotive Group's public shareholders in a deal valuing the company at $16.3 billion, according to Wards Auto (Oct. 15, 2026). Ownership splits roughly 64% Penske to 36% Mitsui once the deal closes, taking one of the largest public dealership groups private.

Use it today: If you're at a Penske store, the short answer for curious customers is that ownership structure changed, not day-to-day operations — inventory, financing, and your deal process stay the same.

Sources: Wards Auto

Hudson Automotive buys Honda of Gallatin, expands Nashville footprint

Hudson Automotive is acquiring Honda of Gallatin from Gill Automotive Group, according to AutoSuccess (Oct. 16, 2026). It's Gill's second Gallatin import-store sale this year, following a February Toyota store sale to Lithia, as Nashville-area dealer groups keep consolidating.

Use it today: If a nearby competitor just got bought, expect a few weeks of manager turnover and price confusion there — flag your CRM to catch anyone cross-shopping that store and follow up fast.

Sources: AutoSuccess
