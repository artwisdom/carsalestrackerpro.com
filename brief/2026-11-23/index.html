Automakers Sue New Jersey Over Warranty Pay Rules

Dealer Ops Daily · Monday, November 23, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Four automakers filed suit to block a New Jersey law requiring full retail rates for warranty repairs, and the average new-car payment is tracking toward a record $840 this month. Both numbers hit your floor differently — one changes your service drive's math, the other changes who walks in ready to buy.

⚖️ Protect the deal

Ford, Honda, Stellantis and VW Sue to Block NJ Warranty Pay Law

Four automakers filed suit against New Jersey over a law requiring manufacturers to pay dealers full retail labor and parts rates on warranty repairs, according to Automotive News (Nov. 20). The suit argues the law oversteps federal preemption; it hasn't been independently reviewed by other outlets yet. If it stands, warranty reimbursement economics shift statewide.

Use it today: If you're in NJ or a state eyeing similar bills, ask your service manager how warranty rates compare to retail — that gap funds, or starves, your fixed-ops bonus pool.

Sources: Automotive News

Lenders' AI Pricing Gaps Could Become Your Problem

A survey found meaningful gaps between loan terms quoted to shoppers and the terms on final paperwork, raising FTC exposure, according to Auto Dealer Today (Nov. 20). Separately, Digital Dealer reports subprime auto delinquencies 60+ days past due hit 6.74% at the end of 2025, per Fitch, up from a 2.58% pandemic low — the backdrop regulators are watching as lenders lean harder on AI underwriting.

Use it today: Before you send a deal to a lender you haven't used in a while, read the final terms line-by-line against the quote — a mismatch becomes the customer's call to the AG, not just yours to defend.

Sources: Auto Dealer Today · Digital Dealer

💰 The desk & the box

Average New-Car Payment Tracks Toward a Record $840

The average new-car payment is on pace to hit $840 in November, with transaction prices near $47,154 and loan rates averaging 6.71%, according to ASOTU's data roundup (Nov. 20); the figures haven't been independently verified against a primary lender dataset. About 30% of trade-ins are carrying negative equity in the same report.

Use it today: Pull your last five deals and check negative equity before you pencil a payment — if it's rolled into the loan without the customer seeing it broken out, that's a menu conversation you want to have before the desk does.

Sources: ASOTU

🚗 Market numbers

Title Fraud Is Slowing Down Used-Car Turns

Vitu's Dave Spencer warned at Used Car Week that slow or fraudulent title transfers are delaying how fast dealers can acquire, retail, or wholesale inventory, according to CBT News (Nov. 20); the claim reflects one vendor's conference remarks and hasn't been independently verified elsewhere.

Use it today: Ask your title clerk how many deals are sitting on a title hold right now — those are units aging on your lot for a paperwork reason, not a pricing one.

Sources: CBT News

🏬 The floor

64 Franchises Changed Hands Last Week, Including 7 CDJR Stores

Automotive News' latest buy-sell tracker counted 64 franchises changing ownership, including seven Chrysler-Dodge-Jeep-Ram stores (Nov. 20); the report hasn't been cross-verified against a second outlet, so treat the exact count as directional. Consolidation keeps moving fastest among domestic brands.

Use it today: If your store is one of the names on that list, or close to one, ask your GM what's changing in pay plans or process in the first 90 days — that's usually where it shows up first.

Sources: Automotive News
