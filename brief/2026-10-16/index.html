Penske Goes Private as New-Car Prices Hit Record

Dealer Ops Daily · Friday, October 16, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Penske Automotive's $16.3B go-private deal caps a week where new-car prices brushed a record and buyers kept shifting to used — know the numbers before your first up walks in.

⚖️ Protect the deal

NHTSA quietly drops its MY2027 safety-testing list

NHTSA published the list of Model Year 2027 vehicles it will crash-test for 5-star safety ratings, per the agency's own release (Oct. 15). No scores exist yet — testing happens months before a rating ever posts.

Use it today: When an early 2027 arrival doesn't have a 5-star rating yet, tell customers it's on NHTSA's testing list, not a red flag — ratings publish after testing wraps, not before.

Sources: NHTSA

💰 The desk & the box

80% of dealers want new revenue partnerships — most don't know how to build one

A new survey from Nation Safe Drivers, covered by Digital Dealer (Oct. 15), found more than 80% of dealership respondents want partnerships with adjacent businesses to create new revenue streams, but most lack a clear path to execute one.

Use it today: Ask your F&I manager what partnership products are on the table this quarter — stores that move on this now will have new menu items before the competition does.

Sources: Digital Dealer / Dealer Magazine

🚗 Market numbers

New-vehicle prices hit $50,562 — and buyers are trading down to used

The average new-vehicle transaction price reached $50,562 in September, just $52 off December's record, according to CBT News (Oct. 15). The same data shows buyers shifting toward used inventory as new prices stay elevated.

Use it today: Lead with the trade-down math on your next new-car up who hesitates at the number — show them a comparable used unit with real savings before they walk.

Sources: CBT News

NHTSA's free complaint database can sharpen your next appraisal

Digital Dealer (Oct. 15) outlines how used-car managers can pull NHTSA's public owner-complaint and recall records by year, make and model — a free layer condition reports and vehicle history reports miss. Used right, it tightens appraisals and reconditioning calls; used sloppily, it creates disclosure risk.

Use it today: Before your next trade appraisal, pull the NHTSA complaint history for that model year and flag anything that affects recon cost or needs disclosure before you pencil the number.

Sources: Digital Dealer / Dealer Magazine · NHTSA.gov (public complaint database)

🏬 The floor

Penske Automotive goes private in $16.3B Mitsui-Penske buyout

Mitsui & Co. and Penske Corp. are buying out the remaining public shares of Penske Automotive, splitting ownership roughly 64-36, according to Wards Auto (Oct. 15); the deal has not yet been independently confirmed by other outlets.

Use it today: Big groups going private often means fresh scrutiny on store-level pay plans and variable comp — if you're at a group store, ask your desk whether benchmarking is coming to your floor next.

Sources: Wards Auto
