OEM Shipments Slip as Allocation Fights Heat Up

Dealer Ops Daily · Wednesday, October 14, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

OEMs took it on the chin yesterday — Stellantis and a Porsche franchise fight both put automaker control over your lot's inventory under the microscope. None of it changes who walks in today, but it changes what you can promise them next month.

⚖️ Protect the deal

Porsche Lawsuit Puts Franchise Allocation Control in the Spotlight

A lawsuit involving Porsche is challenging how much control automakers hold over vehicle allocation under franchise agreements, according to CBT News (Oct. 13); not independently verified elsewhere. The case highlights the cost dealers bear when OEMs dictate which models — and how many — reach a given floor.

Use it today: Next time the desk says "we can't get that trim," ask whether it's a build-out issue or an allocation call from the OEM — knowing the difference helps you set the right expectation with the customer instead of guessing.

Sources: CBT News

🚗 Market numbers

One Hyundai Store Cracks 600 Units a Month With a Retail-First Used Strategy

A Florida Hyundai dealer is moving more than 600 vehicles a month by leaning on stable sales teams and a retail-first approach to used inventory, according to CBT News (Oct. 13); not independently verified elsewhere, even as affordability pressure squeezes buyers nationwide.

Use it today: Pull your own closing ratio by team member for the last 90 days — if your most consistent reps show the highest close rates, make the case to your desk for steadier up-splits instead of pure rotation.

Sources: CBT News

Dealership Buy/Sell Deals Hit Record 462 in the Past Year

The U.S. dealership buy/sell market logged 462 completed deals in the past year, a record pace, according to AutoSuccess (Oct. 13); not independently verified elsewhere, as public retail groups increase acquisition spend and premiums widen for top import franchises, with blue-sky multiples diverging more by state than in past cycles.

Use it today: If your store is part of a group that's been acquisitive, ask your GM where you stand post-deal — consolidation often opens faster paths to desk and F&I seats for proven closers.

Sources: AutoSuccess

🏬 The floor

Stellantis Shipments Fall 1% Globally, 12% in North America

Stellantis shipped 1.3 million vehicles in Q3, down 1% worldwide, with North American shipments dropping 12%, according to CBT News (Oct. 13); not independently verified elsewhere. The CEO rejected calls to split the company's brands despite the regional decline.

Use it today: If you're on a Jeep or Ram floor, check current incoming allocation with your inventory manager this week — tighter North American shipments can mean fewer trims to sell through, so get ahead of customer expectations now.

Sources: CBT News

Porsche Pulls Back From Its All-EV Push, Leans Into Gas and Hybrids

Porsche is slowing its shift to EVs and redirecting investment toward combustion and plug-in hybrid models to protect profitability, according to Automotive Dive (Oct. 13); not independently verified elsewhere, rather than relying on rapid volume growth from electrics.

Use it today: When a customer on the fence about EVs asks if gas is going away, you can honestly say even performance brands are hedging — use that to steer the conversation toward what's in stock today.

Sources: Automotive Dive
