Penske Goes Private as Margins Keep Shrinking

Dealer Ops Daily · Wednesday, November 25, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Penske Automotive filed to go private yesterday, landing in a market where front-end margins keep thinning — net pretax profit fell 11.8% year over year in Q2, according to Digital Dealer. Both stories point to the same shift: stores are leaning harder on the back end to make the math work.

⚖️ Protect the deal

CFPB Joins DOJ on New Anti-Scam Push

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau joined the Department of Justice and other federal agencies to announce new and ongoing efforts against consumer financial fraud, per a CFPB newsroom release. The announcement covers scam protections broadly, not auto-specific rules, but dealers sit in the middle of identity and payment fraud exposure.

Use it today: Flag any mismatched ID, payment method, or trade-title info to your F&I office before you pencil the deal — a documented catch protects your commission and your license if the deal gets scrutinized later.

Sources: CFPB Newsroom

💰 The desk & the box

A Remote F&I Team Just Won a National Innovation Award

BMW and MINI of Pittsburgh's remote finance team took a national Automotive Amplifiers Innovation Award after improving efficiency and increasing PVR, according to AutoSuccess. The store built the setup around centralizing finance work instead of staffing every location individually.

Use it today: If you're working toward the box, ask your F&I manager how your store's deal flow would change with a remote or centralized finance desk — it's a model more stores are testing, and understanding it now puts you ahead when the seat opens up.

Sources: AutoSuccess

🚗 Market numbers

Inventory Is Outgrowing Demand Again

New-vehicle sales were flat quarter over quarter in Q3 2026 while new inventory grew 9%, and used inventory grew in step with a 5% rise in used sales, according to Digital Dealer. A steady sales quarter can mask that kind of buildup until units start aging on the lot.

Use it today: Pull your lot's aging report before the morning meeting and ask the desk which units are past 60 days — those are the ones to push today, before carrying cost eats the deal's profit.

Sources: Digital Dealer

🏬 The floor

Penske Automotive Files to Go Private

Penske Automotive Group filed a Schedule 13E-3 with the SEC on Nov. 24 — the formal disclosure required when a public company moves to go private. The filing, per SEC EDGAR, is the first official step in what's typically a multi-month process, including a shareholder vote and SEC review. No deal terms have been made public yet.

Use it today: If you're on a Penske floor, expect questions from customers and teammates — tell them you'll share real updates from store leadership, not speculation. Don't comment on stock price or valuation; that's not your call and not what customers need from you.

Sources: SEC EDGAR

New-Vehicle Gross Profit Drops to $1,840

The average franchised store's net pretax profit fell 11.8% year over year in Q2, and gross profit on each new vehicle dropped 13.5% to $1,840, according to Digital Dealer. Much of the decline reflects a return from pandemic-era margins, but it's still squeezing the bottom line store by store.

Use it today: Ask your desk which units in today's inventory carry the thinnest front-end gross, and lean harder on trade appraisals and F&I menu presentation on those deals — that's where the margin lives now.

Sources: Digital Dealer
