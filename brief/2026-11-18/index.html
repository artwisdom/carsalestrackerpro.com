NYC's Pricing Ban Puts Your Quote Tool on Notice

Dealer Ops Daily · Wednesday, November 18, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

New York just moved to rein in data-driven price quotes, a seven-store buyout landed in Indianapolis, and a new study says most dealers haven't touched their website setup in years. Here's what actually changes on your floor today.

⚖️ Protect the deal

NYC Votes to Restrict 'Surveillance Pricing'

New York City's council voted to restrict "surveillance pricing" — charging shoppers different prices based on tracked personal data rather than the vehicle or market — according to ASOTU's Daily Download (Nov. 17, 2026); the underlying vote hasn't been independently verified by this newsletter. If it holds, it puts new scrutiny on any online quote tool that personalizes price by browsing history or location.

Use it today: Ask your internet/BDC manager whether your "see your price" tool adjusts the number based on visitor tracking data — if you sell in or near NYC, get that answered before today's online leads come in.

Sources: ASOTU

💰 The desk & the box

Why Rising Delinquency Numbers May Be About to Turn

Auto loan delinquencies are still climbing, but New York Fed researchers say part of the rise is a "pandemic echo" — stimulus-era accounts with inflated credit scores finally aging out of the data, according to Wards Auto (Nov. 17, 2026). As those accounts clear out, researchers expect the delinquency trend to ease.

Use it today: When F&I flags a marginal credit app, ask whether the file is a 2021-2022 vintage — researchers say those accounts are driving the current spike, not necessarily today's buyer's ability to pay.

Sources: Wards Auto / Automotive Dive

🏬 The floor

Five Star Automotive Buys Seven Indianapolis-Area Stores

Five Star Automotive Group acquired seven metro-Indianapolis dealerships and their real estate from Dreyer & Reinbold, according to CBT News (Nov. 17, 2026). The deal adds another multi-store operator to a market that's consolidating fast this year.

Use it today: If you're at one of the affected stores, ask your GM this week about pay plan and process timelines — acquisitions almost always mean a transition period, and getting ahead of the questions beats hearing changes secondhand.

Sources: CBT News

One Company Hosts a Huge Share of Franchise Dealer Websites

Digital Dealer classified all 16,948 live U.S. franchise dealership websites by host company this September and found the market concentrated around a handful of providers, according to Digital Dealer (Nov. 16, 2026). Most dealers only revisit that vendor choice once every few years.

Use it today: Pull up your own store's site on your phone right now and time how long a used-car search takes to load — if it's slow, that's leads lost before the phone ever rings, and it's worth flagging to your internet manager today.

Sources: Digital Dealer / Dealer Magazine
