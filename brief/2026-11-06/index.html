AI Assistants Are Already Picking Your Next Up

Dealer Ops Daily · Friday, November 6, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

AI assistants are now shortlisting dealers before a shopper ever opens your website, and Volvo's third straight global sales drop is a preview of what soft used values look like six months out. Hybrids, meanwhile, keep pulling their own weight for brands that bothered to stock them.

☕ HUDDLE STARTER · read it aloud in 60 seconds

16% — Volvo's global sales dropped 16% over the last three months. That kind of slide usually shows up a few months later as softer used values on the lot.

Ask the team: Does anyone have a Volvo trade-in sitting from before this week that we should run through appraisal again?

💰 The desk & the box

DriveCentric Pairs With Data Axle To Clean Up CRM Data

DriveCentric is integrating Data Axle's verified contact data into its CRM and AI tools, aiming to improve campaign accuracy and compliance across sales and service, according to a company announcement reported by AutoSuccess (Nov. 4).

Use it today: Ask your BDC or desk if your store's CRM is getting this kind of refresh — bad contact data kills follow-up on deals you already worked once.

Sources: AutoSuccess

🚗 Market numbers

Hybrids Are Carrying Sales Growth For Brands That Have Them

Hybrid models are driving most of the sales growth at automakers that offer them, while brands slow to add hybrids are losing ground, according to CBT News (Nov. 5).

Use it today: If your lot has a hybrid sitting past 60 days, put it on today's work list — lead with fuel savings against a shopper cross-shopping a competitor hybrid.

Sources: CBT News

Volvo Global Sales Down 16% As U.S. and China Struggle

Volvo reported global sales fell 16% year-over-year from August through October, citing weaker demand in the U.S. and China, according to Automotive Dive (Nov. 5).

Use it today: If you're sitting on Volvo trade-ins, re-appraise them against today's wholesale numbers before you commit on the desk — soft factory sales often mean softer used values close behind.

Sources: Automotive Dive

Sonic Puts $5M to $7M Behind EchoPark Brand Push

Sonic Automotive plans to spend $5 million to $7 million in Q4 on a brand-awareness campaign for its 12-year-old EchoPark used-car business, aiming to fix what executives call lagging recognition, according to Automotive Dive (Nov. 5).

Use it today: If you compete with an EchoPark store nearby, expect more walk-ins asking about no-haggle pricing — have your own value story ready before the ad spend hits your market.

Sources: Automotive Dive

🏬 The floor

AI Assistants Are Picking Your Next Up Before They Call

Shoppers are now asking AI assistants for "the best Toyota dealer in [city]" and getting a short list before they ever visit a dealer website or submit a lead, according to Digital Dealer (Nov. 5), not yet independently verified by a second outlet.

Use it today: Ask every walk-in and phone-up whether they used an AI tool to research dealers first — if yes, find out what it told them about your floor so the desk can correct bad info.

Sources: Digital Dealer

Audi Millburn Named Best Dealership To Work For In The Country

Automotive News's 2026 Best Dealerships To Work For ranking put Audi Millburn (NJ) at No. 1, with category winners named across brand and size groups.

Use it today: Pull the category list for your region and size class — if a nearby store made it, ask your manager what their pay plan or onboarding looks like, since it's public once it's ranked.

Sources: Automotive News
