GM's $220M Data Scandal, VW Pulls Lease Deal

Dealer Ops Daily · Thursday, November 5, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Two stories today remind you how fast the ground under a deal can shift: GM's data settlement and VW's pulled lease promo both change what you say to today's ups before you even clock in.

⚖️ Protect the deal

GM's $220M Data Settlement Shows Telematics Risk

General Motors agreed to pay $220 million to settle claims that it sold driver behavior data from OnStar-connected vehicles to insurers without clear consent, according to court documents filed by the Texas Attorney General and reported by CBT News (Nov. 4, 2026). The data reportedly included braking and acceleration patterns tied to individual VINs, raising fresh questions about what dealerships disclose when customers opt into connected-car services at delivery.

Use it today: Before next delivery, read the connected-services opt-in screen out loud to the customer instead of just pointing at the tablet — it protects you if data use ever gets questioned later.

Sources: CBT News

AI Governance in Lending Is Now the Industry's Job

Oversight of AI-driven credit decisions is shifting from regulators to dealerships and lenders themselves in 2026, according to AutoSuccess (Nov. 4, 2026). The shift means stores using AI tools for credit scoring or pricing need their own documentation trail if a decision gets challenged.

Use it today: Ask your F&I manager whether your current credit-decision tool keeps an audit log you could hand a regulator today — if nobody knows, that's this week's fix.

Sources: AutoSuccess

💰 The desk & the box

VW Kills Its Sign-and-Drive Lease Deal for 2026

Volkswagen is ending its sign-then-drive lease promotion to close out 2026, a move dealers are pushing back on, according to Automotive News (Nov. 3, 2026). The promo let shoppers walk out with zero money due at signing, and dealers say it was driving walk-in traffic on tight-margin leases.

Use it today: If VW ups are asking about $0-down leases, pivot the conversation to total due-at-signing and payment certainty before they hear it's gone from a competitor's lot.

Sources: Automotive News

🚗 Market numbers

Toyota Recalls 106K Hybrid Land Cruisers for Fuel Leak Risk

Toyota is recalling nearly 106,000 hybrid Land Cruiser SUVs after a fuel-line clip can disconnect and leak gas near a potential ignition source, according to Automotive Dive (Nov. 4, 2026), citing the NHTSA filing. No fires have been confirmed yet, but owners are being told to park outside until fixed.

Use it today: Pull your lot's hybrid Land Cruiser VINs against the recall list before your next test drive — a customer finding out from the news is a lost sale.

Sources: Automotive Dive

🏬 The floor

Dealership Sales Up 8% Through Q3, Presidio Finds

Dealership M&A stayed active through the first nine months of 2026, with transactions up 8% year-over-year and more stores — especially luxury — coming to market, according to Presidio Group data reported by CBT News (Nov. 4, 2026). That's on top of the buy-sell heat we've flagged all year.

Use it today: If ownership talk is in the air at your store, ask the GM where you'd land on the org chart post-sale — better to know now than find out in a memo.

Sources: CBT News
