Amazon Autos Goes From 1 Brand To 35

Dealer Ops Daily · Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Amazon's car-buying platform just went from a single brand to 35, and Nebraska's top court told GM it can't kill a franchise on a whim. Two reminders that the ground under your floor keeps shifting, even when the showroom looks the same.

⚖️ Protect the deal

Nebraska's Supreme Court blocks GM's bid to terminate a Chevy-GMC dealer

Nebraska's Supreme Court ruled GM can't terminate a Chevrolet-GMC dealer's franchise, according to Automotive News (Nov. 23). The court found GM didn't meet the state's good-cause standard required to end a franchise agreement, a rare state-level win for dealer-protection law.

Use it today: If you're weighing a move to another store, ask about its franchise-termination history — strong good-cause laws like Nebraska's protect your paycheck's stability too.

Sources: Automotive News

BMW recalls over 720,000 vehicles a second time for fire risk

BMW issued a second recall on the same vehicles, covering more than 720,000 units, for a water-pump electrical connector that can corrode and short-circuit, according to Automotive Dive (Nov. 23). It's BMW's second fix attempt on this exact part.

Use it today: Pull VINs on any BMW trade-ins or lot units before you promise a delivery date — a flagged VIN can kill today's deal if the fix isn't done first.

Sources: Automotive Dive

💰 The desk & the box

TrueCar brings back The Curve and its Savings Certificate

TrueCar relaunched The Curve and its Savings Certificate program, giving shoppers a locked, dealer-backed discount to bring into the store, according to AutoSuccess (Nov. 23). The timing is meant to compete with deeper Cyber Monday discounting already hitting the market.

Use it today: Treat a Curve certificate as the shopper's floor, not your ceiling — work the trade and the menu the same way you would any other deal.

Sources: AutoSuccess

🚗 Market numbers

UVeye turns the service lane into a fast appraisal tool

AI-powered vehicle scanners installed in service lanes are giving stores a faster way to appraise trade-ins and flag service work while customers wait, according to CBT News (Nov. 23).

Use it today: Ask your service manager if your store runs a scan system — a car in for an oil change is a free appraisal lead you can turn into a trade conversation before it reaches your desk.

Sources: CBT News

🏬 The floor

Amazon Autos grows from one brand to 35

Amazon Autos grew from a single launch brand to 35 as the company sped up its dealer sign-up process, according to Automotive News (Nov. 23). More franchises can now list new-car inventory directly on Amazon's platform.

Use it today: If your store sells one of the 35 brands now on Amazon, find out from your internet manager how those leads route to you before your first Amazon-sourced up walks in.

Sources: Automotive News
