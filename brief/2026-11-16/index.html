721,000 BMWs Recalled - Check Trades Before You Appraise

Dealer Ops Daily · Monday, November 16, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Correction to our November 15 issue: Our November 15 issue named the wrong rule. The FTC’s Stream Auto Outlet complaint alleges violations of Section 5 of the FTC Act.

Sources: FTC complaint

NHTSA just hit nearly three-quarters of a million BMWs with an urgent 'park outside' recall, and Toyota added another 62,000 Tundras to the list - if you're moving trades today, check the VIN before you appraise. A store's technician retention stunt and a lender-trust gap in private-party acquisition round out the floor-level moves worth stealing this week.

⚖️ Protect the deal

NHTSA Flags 721,000 BMWs For 'Park Outside' Fire Risk

NHTSA issued an urgent 'park outside' recall for roughly 721,000 BMW vehicles after finding a fire risk tied to a battery component, according to the agency's Nov. 16 consumer alert. Owners are told not to park in garages until the fix is made.

Use it today: Before you appraise or send a BMW trade to auction, run the VIN through NHTSA's recall lookup - an open 'park outside' flag can kill the deal or the wholesale price if it surfaces later.

Sources: NHTSA (via Google News)

💰 The desk & the box

Private-Party Trade-Ins Are Growing - Stores Without a Process Are Losing Lender Trust

As private-party vehicle acquisition grows, AutoSuccess reports that dealers without a standardized intake process are seeing more surprises on title, condition, and payoff - and losing lender trust as a result. The claim comes from a single trade outlet and hasn't been independently verified.

Use it today: Ask your desk if there's a written checklist for private-party buys - title, lien payoff, and condition disclosure - before you walk a customer's trade-in through acquisition; it's the kind of process gap that kills funding down the line.

Sources: AutoSuccess

🚗 Market numbers

Used EVs Are Outpacing Hybrids and Gas On The Lot, Per Auto Dealer Today

Used EVs are selling faster off the lot than used hybrids or gas vehicles right now, according to Auto Dealer Today's Nov. 13 Dealer Debrief - a single-source claim not yet independently verified. The segment ties the shift to tightening CPO supply across electrified trims.

Use it today: If you're sitting on used EVs, don't default to an early discount - ask your desk whether they're priced against hybrid comps instead of gas comps, since they may be moving faster than either.

Sources: Auto Dealer Today

🏬 The floor

Toyota Recalls 62,000 Tundras - Check Before You Appraise a Trade

Toyota recalled about 62,000 model-year 2022-2023 and 2025-2026 Tundra (non-hybrid) trucks over a component defect, according to a Nov. 13 release from Toyota's newsroom - a primary source. Affected trucks will need a dealer fix, meaning service-drive traffic on vehicles your store may already have on the lot or in trade.

Use it today: Flag any Tundra trade-ins in that year range for recon before resale, and give your service advisor a heads-up - open recalls on these trims are about to bring their owners back through your fixed-ops door.

Sources: Toyota USA Newsroom

One Store Turned Payroll Theater Into a Technician Retention Tool

One dealership is handing techs giant, Publishers Clearing House-style checks at department meetings as a public retention play, according to a Nov. 13 Automotive News write-up - a single-source report not yet independently verified. The move is meant to make payout moments visible instead of buried in a pay stub.

Use it today: Pitch your service manager on a visible payout moment for top techs or top closers this month - a zero-cost morale play borrowed straight from this store's playbook.

Sources: Automotive News (via Google News)
