Used Inventory Floods In as Sales Cool Down

Dealer Ops Daily · Tuesday, October 20, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Used supply jumped 43% while sales slipped 5.5% — more metal, fewer buyers, and more room to negotiate on today's trade-ins. This week we're also tracking how softer demand filters into financing approvals, starting with today's market numbers and a lawsuit that could reshape third-party deal ratings.

⚖️ Protect the deal

NYC Lawsuit Says CarGurus Hid Fees in 'Deal Rating' Scores

CarGurus faces a New York City lawsuit alleging its used-car 'deal rating' system masked add-on fees and inflated apparent savings, according to CBT News (Oct. 19). The suit is unadjudicated and targets how third-party listing sites score deals shoppers see before they ever reach your lot.

Use it today: When an up references a CarGurus 'Great Deal' badge, ask what fees that rating excluded — it reframes the number as their starting point, not yours.

Sources: CBT News

NHTSA Publishes MY2027 Vehicle List for 5-Star Crash Testing

NHTSA released its Model Year 2027 testing list, naming which vehicles will be evaluated for 5-Star Safety Ratings, according to NHTSA.gov (Oct. 19). The list determines which new models can advertise a government safety rating once testing wraps — information shoppers increasingly ask about before they buy.

Use it today: Pull the MY2027 list for your brands and flag which models are pending testing — tell shoppers 'rating not yet published' instead of guessing.

Sources: NHTSA

💰 The desk & the box

55% of Car Shoppers Now Research with AI Before They Visit

55% of people planning to buy a vehicle in the next six months are using AI tools like ChatGPT or Gemini to research first, according to Digital Dealer (Oct. 19), citing an industry survey. Many customers now reach the box with AI-generated price and financing expectations already formed.

Use it today: In the box, ask 'what did you find online already?' early — it surfaces AI-sourced price anchors you need to address before you build the four-square.

Sources: Digital Dealer / Dealer Magazine

🚗 Market numbers

Used Inventory Jumps 43% While Sales Slip 5.5%, Per AutoVision

AutoVision's September/October used-vehicle report shows used supply up 43.4% year over year while sales fell 5.5%, with pickups gaining market share, according to AutoSuccess (Oct. 19), citing AutoVision data. More metal sitting longer means more room to negotiate — and more units aging past turn targets.

Use it today: Check your aging report for units past 60 days and open two or three with a fresh price before the morning meeting — supply says the market won't bail you out.

Sources: AutoSuccess

Hybrids Outselling EVs as GM Cuts a Costly Legacy Part

CBT News (Oct. 19) reports hybrid sales are outpacing EVs among cost-conscious buyers, while GM has dropped dipsticks from some models to cut cost and complexity. The pattern points to shoppers trading range anxiety for fuel savings and simpler maintenance pitches.

Use it today: Lead hybrid conversations with cost-per-mile math instead of range comparisons — it's the argument winning shoppers right now.

Sources: CBT News

🏬 The floor

Three Phone Mistakes Are Costing Your Store Its Appointments

AutoSuccess (Oct. 19) breaks down three common dealership phone and lead-response mistakes that turn warm leads cold before they reach the showroom. The fixes are about speed and specificity in the first callback, not scripts.

Use it today: On your next lead callback, confirm a specific day and time before you hang up — not 'come by whenever.' That one change closes the appointment loop.

Sources: AutoSuccess
