Cox Automotive Confirms VinSolutions Cyber Incident, Says It's Contained

Cox Automotive confirmed a cybersecurity incident affecting its VinSolutions CRM platform, saying the activity has been contained, according to Car Dealership Guy News, Oct. 26 — citing Cox's own statement; not independently verified beyond that confirmation. The company has not disclosed how many dealers or customer records were affected.

Use it today: If your store runs leads or deal data through VinSolutions, ask your GM or IT contact whether your store's instance was confirmed affected, and change any shared CRM login passwords as a precaution.

Sources: Car Dealership Guy News

California Court Lets Dealers' Challenge to Scout Motors' DTC Sales Keep Going

A California court has allowed the California New Car Dealers Association's lawsuit against Scout Motors' direct-to-consumer sales plan to proceed, attorney Len Bellavia told CBT News, Oct. 26. The case centers on whether the VW-owned brand can sell directly to consumers without going through franchised dealers.

Use it today: If you're in a franchise-protection state, this is a live example worth raising with your GM — it's a reminder of why your franchise agreement, and the laws behind it, still carry weight.

Sources: CBT News

💰 The desk & the box

NETSOL's Transcend Retail Gets the Green Light at Mercedes-Benz Stores

Mercedes-Benz USA has approved NETSOL's Transcend Retail platform as a digital retail option for its dealers, according to AutoSuccess, Oct. 26. The platform lets customers build and submit deal structures online before they arrive at the store.

Use it today: If your store carries Mercedes-Benz, ask your desk manager whether Transcend Retail is live yet — reviewing a customer's online deal build before they walk in means you start the negotiation from their number, not from zero.

Sources: AutoSuccess

🚗 Market numbers

LaFontaine Rolls Out AI Vehicle Scanners Across Its Stores

LaFontaine Auto Group is deploying UVeye's AI-powered vehicle-scanning system chain-wide. UVeye's CEO told Automotive Dive, Oct. 26, he expects the tech to eventually show up anywhere cars sit, even grocery store lots. The scanners flag mechanical and cosmetic issues on vehicles in seconds.

Use it today: If your store has one of these scanners, pull the scan report before you appraise a trade — a printed defect list beats an eyeball guess when you're explaining a number to a skeptical customer.

Sources: Automotive Dive

2027 Palisade Hybrid Beats the V-6 on Power and Mileage

Hyundai's 2027 Palisade hybrid delivers 30 mpg combined versus 20 mpg for the V-6 version, while also beating it on power, according to Automotive Dive, Oct. 26.

Use it today: When a Palisade shopper is cross-shopping V-6 trims, lead with the mileage gap — 30 mpg versus 20 mpg is a number they can do the math on in their head.

Sources: Automotive Dive

🏬 The floor

Forecast: October New-Vehicle Sales Slip as Buyers Stretch Loan Terms

A new industry forecast expects October retail deliveries to come in below September as affordability pressure continues, with more buyers turning to longer loan terms to make payments work, according to F&I and Showroom, Oct. 26.

Use it today: If a customer's stuck on payment, walk the desk through term options before you default to stretching the loan — know what a longer term does to their total cost before you pitch it as the fix.

Sources: F&I and Showroom
