What a typical issue looks like

Four to six short stories, each ending with a Use it today line: something specific you can do on the floor that morning, not a summary of the news. Every story links to its sources so you can check it in one tap. The best way to judge it is to read a few recent issues.

How it's made

Dealer Ops Daily is written by AI from public industry sources — trade press, regulators, and market data publishers — and every issue passes automated editorial and accuracy checks before it sends. It isn't financial, legal, or tax advice. If something is wrong, reply to any issue and we'll correct it in the next one.

Who's behind it

It's published by the maker of CarSales Tracker Pro, the deal and commission tracker built for automotive salespeople. The brief is free. Occasionally an issue features the app, and that's always labeled.
