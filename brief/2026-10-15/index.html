Record Payments, Rate Hikes, and a Recall to Watch

Dealer Ops Daily · Thursday, October 15, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Payments are hitting record highs just as rates threaten another hike — and today's compliance, pricing, and ownership-change news all touch what you tell your next up.

⚖️ Protect the deal

Toyota Recalls 106,000 Land Cruisers Over Fuel Line Risk

Toyota is recalling roughly 106,000 model-year 2024-2027 Land Cruisers for a fuel tube connection near the tank that can leak, raising fire risk, according to Toyota's official recall notice (primary source), published Oct. 14.

Use it today: Pull VINs on any Land Cruiser in your inventory or on an upcoming trade before you show it — flag affected units for the dealer fix and disclose the open recall to the customer.

Sources: Toyota USA Newsroom

Report: Many Carvana Google Listings Point to Wrong Addresses

According to a Car Dealership Guy News analysis (not independently verified), a number of Carvana's Google Business Profile listings link to government buildings and other non-Carvana sites instead of the correct location.

Use it today: Check your own store's Google Business Profile today — a wrong address, phone number, or hours listing sends your next up to a competitor before they ever call.

Sources: Car Dealership Guy News

💰 The desk & the box

Record Payments Meet a Possible Second Rate Hike

Average monthly vehicle payments and 84-month loan terms hit new highs, according to CBT News (Oct. 14). Separately, F&I and Showroom reports last month's rate increase — and a possible second hike this month — are adding to affordability pressure across new-car sales.

Use it today: When a payment doesn't pencil, show the 72- vs. 84-month total interest side by side on the menu — it protects your PVR instead of just chasing a lower monthly number.

Sources: CBT News · F&I and Showroom

🚗 Market numbers

New-Vehicle Prices Held Flat in September, KBB Finds

New-vehicle prices stayed roughly flat last month as affordability pressure and limited model availability shaped demand, according to a Kelley Blue Book analysis reported by F&I and Showroom (Oct. 14).

Use it today: Use the flat-pricing data with hesitant shoppers — prices aren't dropping, so today's rate or incentive is the deal, not a future 'wait and see.'

Sources: F&I and Showroom

🏬 The floor

Dealership Buy/Sell Deals Hit a Record 462 in the Past Year

The dealership buy/sell market logged a record 462 transactions over the past year, according to AutoSuccess (not independently verified), with publicly traded groups paying premiums for top import franchises.

Use it today: If your store just changed hands or rumors are flying, ask your GM directly about pay plan continuity — buy/sell waves are exactly when comp plans get rewritten.

Sources: AutoSuccess
