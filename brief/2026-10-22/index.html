NADA's Next Chair Faces an Affordability Reckoning

Dealer Ops Daily · Thursday, October 22, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

NADA named its 2027 leadership team yesterday, and the incoming chair takes over right as affordability and trust questions are reshaping how every floor sells a car.

⚖️ Protect the deal

NADA Picks Its Next Chair — and a Vice Chair Lines Up for 2028

National Automobile Dealers Association announced Sandy Fitzgerald Angello will become chairman in 2027, only the second woman to hold the role, according to ASOTU's Oct. 21 coverage of the announcement. Jeff Weber moves into vice chair, positioning him for the top seat in 2028. The incoming team takes over as affordability, regulation, and customer trust dominate the association's agenda.

Use it today: If a customer brings up industry trust, mention that the group representing your store just put affordability and transparency at the top of next year's agenda — it's a real answer, not a deflection.

Sources: ASOTU YouTube

💰 The desk & the box

GM's Super Cruise Revenue Jumped 60% in Q3

General Motors' Super Cruise driver-assist system posted a 60% year-over-year revenue jump in the third quarter, according to CEO Mary Barra's shareholder letter as cited by Automotive Dive (Oct. 21, 2026). Barra said trucks will be a major driver of incremental Super Cruise adoption and digital revenue in 2027.

Use it today: Start noticing which trims and packages attach tech subscriptions best on your lot — that attach rate is becoming a real revenue line the desk watches, not just a sales pitch.

Sources: Automotive Dive

🚗 Market numbers

Hertz Expands Three-Day Test Drives to 300+ Locations

Hertz is rolling its TestDrive+ program out to more than 300 locations, letting used-car shoppers keep a vehicle for up to three days before buying, according to ASOTU's Oct. 21 coverage of the rollout; not independently verified beyond the company's own account. Buyers who purchase get the daily rental fee waived.

Use it today: Ask your next fence-sitter if they'd rather live with the car for a weekend than take one more lap of the lot — more windshield time closes more deals than another walkaround.

Sources: ASOTU YouTube

🏬 The floor

VIP Auto Group Dumps Outside Software for Custom AI Tools

VIP Auto Group is replacing several third-party software platforms with AI tools built in-house, according to CBT News (Oct. 21, 2026); not independently verified beyond the dealer group's own account. The move comes as more stores weigh buying off-the-shelf AI products versus building their own.

Use it today: Ask your GM which vendor tools your store pays for every month that a custom build could replace — it's a question that shows you're thinking past your own pay plan.

Sources: CBT News
