Trade-In Debt Returns to the Showroom Floor

Dealer Ops Daily · Wednesday, October 28, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

New-vehicle sales look steady on the surface, but a bigger trade-in debt load is building in the lane underneath it. Here's what changes at your desk today.

⚖️ Protect the deal

AI Tools in F&I Carry Compliance Risk You Can't Ignore

F&I and Showroom's Oct. 26 report, not independently verified elsewhere, warns that AI now touches pricing, lead-scoring, and F&I workflows — and that an algorithm's recommendation doesn't erase a human's duty to document why a customer got a given rate or add-on.

Use it today: Before a system-generated rate or add-on goes on the four-square, ask the desk what the business reason is — you'll need it if a customer ever disputes the deal.

Sources: F&I and Showroom (FI Magazine)

💰 The desk & the box

A New Wave of Trade-In Debt Is Building in the Lane

New-vehicle sales volume has held roughly flat year over year, but Digital Dealer's Oct. 27 report, not yet independently verified, says a bigger trade-in debt load is building beneath that calm headline number as more upside-down trades reach the lane than the sales figures suggest.

Use it today: Pull yesterday's trade appraisals and flag anything financed in the last two years before you quote a payoff out loud — confirm the number with the desk first.

Sources: Digital Dealer / Dealer Magazine

Ally Says Its 'Approve Everything' Strategy Is Paying Off

Ally Financial is seeing growth in auto loan and lease originations after telling dealers to submit every application rather than pre-screening for approval odds, according to Automotive Dive's Oct. 27 report. The lender says the wider funnel, not looser standards, is driving the gain.

Use it today: Stop guessing who'll get declined — submit the deal and let the box decide, and ask your F&I manager if your store has adopted Ally's pass-everything policy yet.

Sources: Automotive Dive / WardsAuto

🚗 Market numbers

AI Shopping Tools Are Raising the Bar on Price Transparency

CarEdge's CEO tells CBT News that as more shoppers use AI to research and compare vehicles before they arrive, dealers need to rethink how pricing is presented online and on the lot — these tools surface discrepancies that used to go unnoticed.

Use it today: Check your own listing against what an AI assistant pulls up for the same VIN — fix any mismatch before a customer walks in holding the discrepancy.

Sources: CBT News

Reynolds and Reynolds Puts the CPO Experience Center Stage

Reynolds and Reynolds announced its AutoVision platform will spotlight the dealer-experience side of certified pre-owned retail at Used Car Week, per the company's Oct. 27 announcement covered by CBT News.

Use it today: If your store runs CPO, ask your used-car manager whether AutoVision or a similar tool is on the roadmap — worth knowing before a rollout lands on your desk.

Sources: CBT News

🏬 The floor

Lani Automotive Group Adds an Iowa Store to Its Portfolio

Lani Automotive Group acquired Mount Pleasant Chevrolet GMC Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram in Iowa from Coleman Automotive Group, according to CBT News's Oct. 27 report, not yet independently verified by other outlets.

Use it today: If your store ever changes hands, ask your GM early about pay plan continuity and title transfer timing — those are the two things that actually affect your next check.

Sources: CBT News
