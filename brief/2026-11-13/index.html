Stellantis Puts a Fixer in Charge of U.S. Dealers

Dealer Ops Daily · Friday, November 13, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Stellantis just put Tim Kuniskis — the exec known for aggressive Dodge and Ram incentive plays — in charge of all U.S. sales and dealer relations, replacing the outgoing Dodge CEO. If you're on a Stellantis point, expect faster allocation and incentive shifts than you're used to this week.

⚖️ Protect the deal

Toyota Recalls 314,000 Vehicles For Takata Airbags

Toyota is recalling about 314,000 U.S. vehicles because the front passenger airbag, built with a Takata inflator, can rupture, according to Toyota's Nov. 12 recall notice (primary source). The recall covers older Toyota and Lexus models still moving through trade-in lanes right now.

Use it today: Before you appraise any Toyota or Lexus trade older than model-year 2016, run the VIN through NHTSA's recall lookup — an open Takata campaign can stall your title work and kill the deal at delivery.

Sources: Toyota USA Newsroom

FTC Fines Family Dollar $63M Over Price Mismatches

The FTC and 17 states reached a $63 million settlement with Family Dollar over allegations it routinely charged shoppers more at the register than the shelf price, according to the FTC's Nov. 12 announcement (primary source). It's the latest price-transparency case built on the same ad-pricing statute dealers answer to.

Use it today: Walk your lot's window stickers against whatever's quoted on your website or third-party listings — a mismatch here is the same violation that just cost Family Dollar $63 million.

Sources: FTC Press Releases

💰 The desk & the box

AI-Faked Pay Stubs Are Now a Lender Problem

Generative AI tools can convincingly fabricate pay stubs, utility bills, and driver's licenses, eroding lenders' traditional income checks, according to Digital Dealer, Nov. 12 (not yet independently verified). Dealers and lenders are starting to pair document review with new verification layers to catch synthetic paperwork before funding.

Use it today: If a stip package looks too clean — perfect formatting, no bank-logo artifacts — flag it to your F&I manager before you push for funding; a deal kicked back post-funding costs you the whole commission.

Sources: Digital Dealer / Dealer Magazine

Old 'Out of Trust' Cases Are a Warning for New Managers

F&I and Showroom revisited past floor-plan fraud cases where dealers sold vehicles without paying off the flooring lender, a violation known as being "out of trust," according to the outlet's Nov. 11 piece (not yet independently verified). The cases show how fast these violations turn into criminal exposure, not just a credit-line problem.

Use it today: If you're eyeing the desk, ask your GM how your store reconciles floor-plan payoffs against units sold — understanding that process now is how you avoid inheriting someone else's out-of-trust mess later.

Sources: F&I and Showroom (FI Magazine)

🚗 Market numbers

Pricier Trims Push Averages Up While Buyers Chase CPO

Average new-vehicle transaction prices keep climbing as October sales skewed toward bigger, pricier models, even as budget-conscious shoppers bought more economical trims, according to F&I and Showroom, Nov. 11. CBT News reports dealers are leaning harder on certified pre-owned inventory as affordability pressure squeezes new-car shoppers this fall, Nov. 12.

Use it today: When a new-car up blinks at the payment, pivot straight to your CPO wall — frame the warranty and lower payment as the smarter buy, not the consolation prize.

Sources: F&I and Showroom (FI Magazine) · CBT News

🏬 The floor

Stellantis Hands Kuniskis the U.S. Sales and Dealer Desk

Stellantis named Tim Kuniskis to oversee U.S. sales and dealer networks, replacing the outgoing Dodge CEO, according to Automotive News, Nov. 12 (not yet independently verified elsewhere). Kuniskis built his reputation on aggressive dealer-facing incentive and allocation moves at Dodge and Ram.

Use it today: If you're on a Stellantis point, expect faster allocation and incentive changes than usual over the next few weeks — ask your GM what's already landed from the new regime before you quote stale numbers to a shopper.

Sources: Automotive News (via Google News)
