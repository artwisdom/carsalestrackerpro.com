Hertz Shuts Down Its Used-Car Showrooms

Dealer Ops Daily · Wednesday, November 11, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Hertz is closing every Hertz Car Sales storefront to push used-vehicle retail fully online — a sign that even legacy remarketing is going digital. That shift, plus a quiet search-engine workaround at Carvana, shows how much of today's used-car fight is happening on a screen before a customer ever hits your lot.

🚗 Market numbers

Hertz Is Closing Every Hertz Car Sales Location

Car Dealership Guy News reports, in a Nov. 9 scoop, that Hertz plans to close every Hertz Car Sales retail location as it pushes used-vehicle sales entirely online. The move — not yet independently verified — would end in-person retail at the rental giant's remarketing arm, shifting more off-rental inventory into wholesale and online-only channels.

Use it today: Ask your desk if off-rental units are showing up more on your trade board — Hertz's inventory has to land somewhere, and it may be wholesale lanes near you.

Sources: Car Dealership Guy News

Carvana's 'Closed' Google Listings Still Funnel Traffic To Its National Site

Car Dealership Guy News found that several Carvana locations marked 'permanently closed' on Google still carry active business listings that redirect shoppers to Carvana's national site — a gap the outlet says hasn't been independently verified by Carvana. The workaround keeps local search traffic flowing to the brand even after a storefront shuts down.

Use it today: Check your own store's Google Business listing today — confirm hours, inventory link, and redirects are accurate, since a stale listing can lose you a walk-in before they ever call.

Sources: Car Dealership Guy News

💰 The desk & the box

Managers Are Split On The Market — Use Value, Not Price, To Close

CBT News reports that dealers and managers are reading today's market very differently — some call it strong, others call it rough — and argues reps who close on total value, not just the lowest number, are winning more deals in that split environment.

Use it today: When a customer stalls on price, pivot to total value — trade equity, service contract, maintenance plan — before you cut the number again. Ask your desk which value levers they want pushed hardest today.

Sources: CBT News

Remote F&I Managers Are Easing Busy-Day Backups

AutoSuccess reports that Assurant Dealer Services' Gary Bosses says remote F&I managers are filling in during peak traffic, letting stores keep signing deals when the in-house office backs up — a staffing model Assurant describes as part of the regular rotation, not just a backup plan.

Use it today: If your store runs remote F&I coverage, ask to shadow a session — it's becoming a real path toward the box, and knowing the workflow now puts you ahead when a desk or F&I seat opens up.

Sources: AutoSuccess

🏬 The floor

Dynatron: Nearly 2,500 Dealers Just Got Benchmarked On Repair Orders

CBT News reports that Dynatron analyzed customer-pay repair order data from nearly 2,500 dealerships to benchmark Fixed Ops performance against stores of similar size — a comparison most dealers haven't run on their own numbers, according to CBT News's coverage.

Use it today: Ask your service drive how your store's customer-pay RO numbers stack up against similar-sized dealers — a weak repair history on a trade can mean a lower appraisal, so flag it before you pencil the deal.

Sources: CBT News
