⚖️ Protect the deal

FTC Shuts Down $106.8M Debt Relief Scheme That Impersonated Lenders

A federal court permanently banned the operators of an alleged debt relief scheme that impersonated banks, credit card companies and government agencies to target seniors and veterans, according to the FTC's October 22 press release, the primary source on the action. The court entered a $106.8 million judgment and ordered the defendants to turn over assets.

Use it today: If a customer mentions a "debt relief" company that called claiming to be their lender, tell them to call the lender's number on their own statement before signing anything — these schemes mirror real company names.

Sources: FTC Press Releases

💰 The desk & the box

VinSolutions Builds Fullpath's AI Data Right Into the CRM

VinSolutions is embedding Fullpath's customer data platform and AI insights directly into its CRM, according to AutoSuccess (Oct. 22); the report has not been independently verified elsewhere. The integration surfaces enriched customer profiles and deal-priority scores on the same screen reps already use, cutting tool-switching for desk and BDC teams.

Use it today: Ask your desk manager if your store runs VinSolutions and whether Fullpath is live — if it is, the AI priority score on a lead is a faster read than your gut on who to call first.

Sources: AutoSuccess

🚗 Market numbers

California New-Vehicle Sales Post First Quarterly Gain of 2026

California's new-vehicle market posted its first overall quarterly gain of the year in Q3, driven by a jump in hybrid sales, according to F&I and Showroom (Oct. 21); the figures have not yet been independently verified elsewhere.

Use it today: If hybrids have been a hard sell on your lot, pull your own Q3 hybrid numbers against last quarter — California's bounce suggests the objection is softening, not just the price.

Sources: F&I and Showroom (FI Magazine)

🏬 The floor

Group 1 Buys 10 Hennessy Stores in Atlanta for $1.3 Billion

Group 1 Automotive is acquiring ten Hennessy dealerships in the Atlanta market for $1.3 billion, according to Automotive News (Oct. 21); the deal has not yet been independently confirmed by other outlets. It's one of the largest single-market dealer group acquisitions reported this year.

Use it today: If you're on a Hennessy floor, expect pay-plan and process questions this week — ask your GM directly what changes and when, rather than guessing from the rumor mill.

Sources: Automotive News (via Google News)

FCA Recalls Nearly 266,000 Ram ProMaster Vans Over Power Steering Loss

FCA US is recalling close to 266,000 Ram ProMaster vans because water intrusion can corrode the wiring harness and disable electric power steering assist, according to Wards Auto's report on the recall filing (Oct. 22).

Use it today: If you've got a ProMaster on your used lot or in a fleet deal, check the VIN against the recall before you deliver it — a disabled steering assist is a liability you don't want following the sale.

Sources: Automotive Dive / Wards Auto
