Buy-Sell Market Keeps Running Hot This Fall

Dealer Ops Daily · Wednesday, November 4, 2026 · 5-minute read

Running the morning meeting? Save card

Buy-sell activity keeps climbing, the industry's pushing Congress to lock in the Chinese-vehicle ban, and F&I is rethinking how it treats high-mileage trades. Here's what to carry onto the floor this morning.

⚖️ Protect the deal

Industry Group Pushes Congress to Make Chinese Vehicle Ban Permanent

According to CBT News (Nov. 3), an auto industry trade group is urging Congress to make the current ban on Chinese-made vehicles permanent before the legislative session ends in December. A Senate committee is reviewing the measure now; it has not passed.

Use it today: If a customer asks about Chinese automakers entering the U.S., tell them straight: Congress hasn't decided whether to keep the current ban in place past this session.

Sources: CBT News

💰 The desk & the box

AI's Next F&I Job Might Be Coaching You, Not the Customer

According to AutoSuccess (Nov. 3), F&I trainer Josh Danielson argues the bigger near-term opportunity for AI in the box is coaching behind the scenes — tracking menu presentation, compliance language, and consistency across every F&I manager on the floor.

Use it today: If you're angling for the box, ask your manager whether your store reviews recorded menu presentations — that's the kind of data these coaching tools are starting to use.

Sources: AutoSuccess

High-Mileage Trade-Ins Need a Different F&I Script

According to AutoSuccess (Nov. 3), F&I trainer Rick Tudor says dealers selling vehicles with 100,000+ miles are leaving protection-plan and service-retention revenue on the table by using the same pitch they'd give on a near-new car.

Use it today: On your next high-mileage trade or used unit, ask the box to walk the customer through protection built for older vehicles — don't let the menu default to the near-new script.

Sources: AutoSuccess

🚗 Market numbers

Hybrids and Retail Demand Drove October Gains for the Big Four Importers

According to Automotive News (Nov. 3), Toyota, Honda, Hyundai, and Kia all posted October sales gains, with hybrid models and stronger retail — non-fleet — demand cited industrywide as the main drivers.

Use it today: If you're on an import lot, ask the desk which hybrid trims are moving fastest right now — that's your opener with anyone cross-shopping mileage against EV sticker shock.

Sources: Automotive News (via Google News)

🏬 The floor

Buy-Sell Market Keeps Running Hot — On the Ground and On Wall Street

An Iowa auto group and a separate single-point store both changed hands this week, part of what F&I and Showroom (Nov. 2) calls a deluge of buy-sell transactions making 2026 a record year for dealership M&A. Separately, a Schedule 13D/A filed with the SEC on Nov. 3 shows a major shareholder adjusting its stake in Carvana.

Use it today: If you're building toward ownership or a GM seat, pull the F&I and Showroom report and see what multiples recent buy-sells are landing at in your region.

Sources: F&I and Showroom (FI Magazine) · SEC EDGAR

U.S. Bank Rolls Out Real-Time Payment Rails for Dealership Back Offices

According to AutoSuccess (Nov. 3), U.S. Bank and Updation are partnering to let dealerships centralize accounts-payable and move from paper checks to real-time digital payments through bank-led FBO accounts, tightening controls without adding back-office headcount.

Use it today: If your store still cuts paper checks to vendors, flag this to your office manager — faster AP could mean faster reimbursement on your deal-related expenses too.

Sources: AutoSuccess
