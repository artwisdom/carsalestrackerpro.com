What to include

A little context helps us help you faster:

  • Support & bugs — tell us whether you're on the web tracker or the iOS app, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. A screenshot is worth a thousand words.
  • Feature requests — describe the problem you're trying to solve, not just the feature. It helps us find the best fix.
  • Corrections — if a number or claim in one of our guides looks off for your store or region, point us to the article and tell us what you're seeing. We take accuracy seriously and update guides when they're wrong.
  • Press & partnerships — reach out at the same address and we'll route it to the right place.

A note on your data

The free web tracker keeps your deal data in your own browser — we don't have it on a server, so we can't look up your numbers or recover them for you. If you're switching devices or browsers, use the built-in Export data (JSON) and Import data buttons in the tracker's settings. Full details are in our privacy policy.
