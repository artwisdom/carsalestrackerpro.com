Why percentages mislead

A 30% plan can pay less than a 25% plan.

The headline rate is only one variable. Pack comes out before the percentage. Minis replace the percentage on thin deals. A recoverable draw can reduce the check after you have already earned the commission. Volume bonuses may start at a threshold you rarely reach, while a smaller rate with lower pack can pay more on every unit.

That is why our tools ask for the whole structure. If Plan A pays 30% after an $1,000 pack and Plan B pays 25% after a $400 pack, Plan B wins on a $2,000-gross deal: $400 versus $300. The pay-plan comparison calculator lets you test your own figures across an entire month.

Quick answers

Before you run the numbers.

How do I calculate commission on a car deal?
Start with front-end gross, subtract the pack in your written pay plan, then multiply the remaining commissionable gross by your rate. If that result is below the plan's mini, use the mini instead. Back-end participation, spiffs, volume bonuses, and draw recovery sit outside that basic line.
Is the free tracker storing my customer data on a server?
No. The web tracker uses your browser's local storage. That improves privacy, but it also means we cannot recover your data. Use the built-in export option to keep a backup before clearing browser data or moving devices.
Can the calculator tell me exactly what payroll owes me?
It can reproduce the assumptions you enter, but it cannot see your dealership's books or written plan. Treat the result as a reconciliation estimate. Your signed plan, deal accounting, and official commission statement determine the final amount.
Are the guides financial or employment advice?
No. They are educational resources for understanding common pay-plan mechanics. Store practices and laws vary, so ask your manager, payroll department, accountant, or qualified employment professional when a decision affects your rights or taxes.
