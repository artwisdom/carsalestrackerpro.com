What Michael builds and maintains

CarSales Tracker Pro began as a focused answer to a practical dealership problem: deal details live in too many places, and commission math is easy to lose between a desk log, a phone note, a payroll statement, and memory. The product keeps customer, vehicle, delivery, paperwork, gross, and commission information together so a salesperson can see the month as it develops.

Michael maintains the iOS product, the local-first web tracker, the commission calculator, the pay-plan comparison calculator, and the publishing system behind the site's guides. He also operates Andromeda Kinship, an independent software development and marketing studio.

What this background does—and does not—mean

Michael's role is product development and publishing. He is not presented here as your attorney, accountant, payroll professional, or dealership's compensation authority. Car-sales pay plans are private agreements, and their terms vary by store, state, role, and manufacturer. The calculators model the figures a reader enters; they do not independently determine what an employer owes.

That boundary matters. The site is designed to help a salesperson understand the arithmetic, ask better questions, and keep a better independent record. Your signed pay plan, official deal accounting, payroll statement, and applicable law govern the real result.

Publishing responsibility

Michael is the named publisher responsible for what appears on this site. Research, drafting, editing, and validation may use AI-assisted tools, especially to compare examples and check repeated calculations. Automation is not treated as a source, and it is not used as a substitute for a verifiable fact. Numerical examples must show their assumptions; claims that depend on an outside source should link to that source; and illustrative industry ranges must be labeled as ranges rather than universal rules.

The full process—including calculator formulas, update standards, corrections, advertising, and the role of AI—is documented in the editorial policy.

Corrections and contact

Dealership practices differ, and the best correction often comes from a reader holding a real pay plan or commission statement. If a guide describes a practice poorly, a formula produces an unexpected result, or a page needs clearer assumptions, email support@andromedakinship.com. Include the page URL, the sentence or calculation in question, and enough non-confidential context to reproduce the issue.

Do not send customer names, phone numbers, deal jackets, pay statements, or other private dealership information. A redacted example or a description of the plan term is usually enough.
