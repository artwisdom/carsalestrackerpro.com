About CarSales Tracker Pro

CarSales Tracker Pro is a deal, delivery, and commission tracker built for one specific person: the automotive salesperson who wants to know exactly what they're earning — before payday, not after.

Why this exists

Most car salespeople track their month on a whiteboard, a notes app, or in their head. Then payday comes and the check doesn't match the number they expected — because pack came out, half the deals were minis, or a recoverable draw ate into it. The information to avoid that surprise exists on every deal; it's just never in one place.

CarSales Tracker Pro was built to be that one place. Log each car's gross and commission, watch month-to-date pay climb in real time, keep pending deliveries and C.O.D.s from slipping, and walk into payday with an independent record. The web version is free and keeps data in the browser; the iOS app adds the full mobile workflow and additional subscription features.

How our guides are written

The articles in the guide library exist to help car salespeople complete a task: understand commission, read a plan, check a statement, compare two structures, or build a repeatable floor habit. The full process is public in the editorial policy and calculator methodology. The practical standard is:

  • Show the assumptions. Worked examples name the gross, pack, rate, mini, and other inputs so a reader can check the arithmetic.
  • Ranges, not false precision. Pay varies by store, region, role, and product mix. Illustrative ranges are labeled as examples rather than presented as universal rules.
  • Useful before it's promotional. A guide has to stand on its own. If it happens to point you toward our free calculator or tracker, that's because the tool genuinely helps with what the article is about — not the other way around.
  • Transparent about automation. AI may assist research organization, drafting, editing, markup, and repeated calculation checks. It is not treated as a factual source, and Michael remains the named publisher responsible for the page.
  • Correctable. Material updates receive a new date. Readers can report a specific sentence, source, link, or formula to a real support inbox.

Our guides are educational. They are not financial, tax, or employment advice, and your actual pay is governed by your own dealership's plan — see our disclaimer for the full picture.

Your data stays yours

The free web tracker stores everything you enter in your own browser. It doesn't sync to a server we control, and we don't ask you to create an account to use it. The full details are in our privacy policy.
