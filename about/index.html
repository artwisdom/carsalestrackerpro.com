About CarSales Tracker Pro

CarSales Tracker Pro is a deal, delivery, and commission tracker built for one specific person: the automotive salesperson who wants to know exactly what they're earning — before payday, not after.

Why this exists

Most car salespeople track their month on a whiteboard, a notes app, or in their head. Then payday comes and the check doesn't match the number they expected — because pack came out, half the deals were minis, or a recoverable draw ate into it. The information to avoid that surprise exists on every deal; it's just never in one place.

We built CarSales Tracker Pro to be that one place. Log each car's gross and commission, watch your month-to-date pay climb in real time, keep your pipeline of pending deliveries and C.O.D.s from slipping, and walk into payday already knowing the number. The web version is free and keeps your data in your own browser; the iOS app adds push reminders and the ATLAS AI assistant.

Who builds it

CarSales Tracker Pro is designed and maintained by Andromeda Kinship, an independent software studio that builds focused, single-purpose tools for people who do real work for a living. We're a small team, which is deliberate: it means every feature is decided by someone who has actually read the support email, and every guide is written by someone accountable for whether it's correct.

We're reachable. Questions, corrections, and feature requests all go to a real inbox at support@andromedakinship.com — see our contact page.

How our guides are written

The articles in our blog exist to answer the questions car salespeople actually type into a search box: how commission is calculated, what a mini deal is, how pay plans are structured, how much reps really make. We hold them to a simple standard:

  • Grounded in how dealerships actually pay. Every figure — pack ranges, commission percentages, mini amounts, draw structures — reflects how front-line automotive pay plans commonly work, described in plain English rather than sales-manager jargon.
  • Ranges, not false precision. Pay varies enormously by store, region, and product mix. Where we give numbers, we give realistic ranges and explain what moves them, instead of pretending there's one right answer.
  • Useful before it's promotional. A guide has to stand on its own. If it happens to point you toward our free calculator or tracker, that's because the tool genuinely helps with what the article is about — not the other way around.
  • Kept current. When pay-plan norms or the tools change, we update the guides and note the revision.

Our guides are educational. They are not financial, tax, or employment advice, and your actual pay is governed by your own dealership's plan — see our disclaimer for the full picture.

Your data stays yours

The free web tracker stores everything you enter in your own browser. It doesn't sync to a server we control, and we don't ask you to create an account to use it. The full details are in our privacy policy.
